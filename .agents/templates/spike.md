# Spike: [Question or Problem]

> Optional prompts: keep only sections that improve the decision.

## Objective
What question are we answering, why does it matter, and which decision could change?

## Hypotheses
What do we expect to happen, and why?

## Experiments
What did we actually do, under which conditions? How can results be reproduced?

## Findings
What worked, what failed, and what evidence supports each conclusion? Distinguish observations from interpretations.

## Remaining Uncertainty
What is still unknown? Which unknowns could change the decision?

## Recommendation
What changed because of the findings? What should we do next, and why?
