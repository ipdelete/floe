# Design: [Problem or Feature]

> Optional prompts: keep only sections that improve the decision.

## Problem
What problem are we solving, and why does it matter?

## Goals and Non-Goals
What outcomes matter? What is outside scope?

## Constraints and Assumptions
What limits our choices? Which assumptions could invalidate the design?

## Evidence
Which findings support the proposed choices, and how? Link to the evidence.

## Proposed Design
Describe architecture, components, interactions, and important behaviors.

## Alternatives and Trade-offs
What else was considered, and why was it rejected?

## Decisions
What was decided, why, and by whom where relevant?

## Validation
What tests, measurements, and invariants establish that the design works?

## Open Questions
What remains uncertain? What would trigger reconsideration?

## Implementation Learnings
What did implementation reveal? Which findings changed assumptions or decisions? Omit if none.
